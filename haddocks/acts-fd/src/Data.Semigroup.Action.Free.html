-- Note on terminology: \"free\" here means /free/ in the free-algebra, left-adjoint-to-forgetful
-- sense (the universal action generated freely by a set of generators @x@). That is unrelated to
-- the notion of \"free\" (trivial stabilizers, i.e. injective orbit maps) used in
-- 'Data.Semigroup.Action.Class.Torsor'\'s documentation.

-- | The free action of a 'Monoid' @m@ on a set of generators @x@: @'Free' m x@ pairs every
-- generator with the action accumulated on it so far.
--
-- 'act' prepends to the @m@ component, making @'Free' m x@ the free /left/ @m@-action; 'ract'
