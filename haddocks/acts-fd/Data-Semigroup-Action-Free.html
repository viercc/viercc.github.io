Free action of a monoid

newtype Free m x Source #

The free action of a Monoid m on a set of generators x: Free m x pairs every generator with the action accumulated on it so far.

act prepends to the m component, making Free m x the free left m-action; ract appends to it, making it \(simultaneously\) the free right m-action — much like Regular carries both the left and the right regular action of the same monoid at once.

Note: this is a newtype around (m, x) rather than using the plain tuple, since (m, x) already has an unrelated Action instance (acting independently on each component).

Free m has a Monad instance by being the free algebra of left m-action. This Monad is exactly the Writer monad (,) m. Note that the monad instance *could* be a free right m-action monad switching the order of monoid value accumulation, but what provided here is the left one.

Free action of a semigroup

newtype FreeSemi s x Source #

The free action of a Semigroup s (not necessarily a Monoid) on a set of generators x: obtained by adjoining an identity to s — Nothing, using the existing Semigroup s => Semigroup (Maybe s)/Monoid (Maybe s) instances from Data.Maybe — and applying the Free construction above.

Unlike pureFree, pureFreeSemi needs no constraint on s at all: that is the entire point of adjoining the identity rather than requiring one already exist.

FreeSemi s has a Monad instance by being the free algebra of left s-action. This Monad is exactly the Writer monad (,) (Maybe s). Note that the monad instance *could* be a free right s-action monad switching the order of monoid value accumulation, but what provided here is the left one.
